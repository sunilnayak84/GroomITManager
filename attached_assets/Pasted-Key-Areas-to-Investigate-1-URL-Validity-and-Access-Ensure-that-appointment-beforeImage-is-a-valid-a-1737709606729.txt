Ensure Firebase Storage has proper CORS settings. Missing configurations can block the image in browsers.
Debugging Steps:
Check CORS settings in Firebase Console:
Go to Firebase Console > Storage > Rules.
Ensure your app's origin is added to the CORS configuration:
